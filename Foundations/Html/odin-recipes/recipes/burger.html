Burger

Description

A burger is a delicious sandwich made with a juicy patty, fresh vegetables, cheese, and sauces inside a soft bun.

It is quick to prepare and makes a satisfying meal for lunch or dinner. Serve with fries or a cold drink.

Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 burger patties
  • 2 cheese slices
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup

Steps

  1. Cook the burger patties until fully done.
  2. Lightly toast the burger buns.
  3. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the buns.
  4. Add lettuce, the cooked patty, cheese, tomato, and onion.
  5. Cover with the top bun.
  6. Serve immediately with fries.
