Pizza

Description

Pizza is one of the world's most popular dishes, featuring a crispy crust topped with tomato sauce, melted cheese, and your favorite toppings.

It is perfect for parties, family dinners, or a quick homemade meal. Serve hot with chili flakes and oregano for extra flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza base
  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 1½ cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • ½ cup bell peppers, chopped
  • ½ cup onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the pizza base.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and vegetables.
  4. Sprinkle oregano and chili flakes.
  5. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the cheese melts and the crust is golden.
  6. Slice and serve hot.
